About Video Converter

Drop in MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, AVI, FLV, WMV, M4V, 3GP, MPG, TS, M2TS, OGV, VOB or GIF and pick an output: MP4, WebM, MKV, MOV, AVI, GIF, MPG, TS, WMV or OGV. Every file is identified by its actual contents, not its extension.

Remux first. When the video and audio inside your file are already legal in the target container — an H.264 MKV going to MP4, say — the streams are copied, not re-encoded: it takes seconds and loses nothing. Only when a stream genuinely has to change (WMV to MP4, anything to GIF, a lower resolution) does the tool re-encode, and it tells you roughly how long that will take before you start, so you can trade time for a smaller resolution or a faster preset.

The conversion runs on ffmpeg compiled to WebAssembly, entirely inside this tab: your files never leave your browser. The ~31 MB engine downloads once on first use and is cached afterwards. It runs on a single CPU thread, so re-encoding is slow compared with a desktop app — the estimate is there so you know what you are in for.

📱 iPhone / iPad: Safari limits how much memory a tab may use, so large files (roughly 200 MB and up) can fail or reload the page here. For big conversions use a desktop browser.
